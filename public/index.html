FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What can I expect when I visit Divine Inspiration Church for the first time?

A: When you visit Divine Inspiration Church, you can expect a warm welcome from our church family. Our services are centered around worship, prayer, and the teaching of God’s Word. Whether you’re new to faith or a lifelong believer, you’ll find a supportive community and an atmosphere of reverence and fellowship.

Q: What are the service times at Divine Inspiration Church?

A: Our main worship service takes place on Sunday mornings at 10:00 AM. We also offer Morning Prayer at 8:00 AM, Sunday School at 9:00 AM, and a midweek Bible Study on Wednesdays at 7:00 PM. We welcome you to join us at any of these services!

Q: What is your church’s stance on baptism?

A: At Divine Inspiration Church, we believe that baptism is an important step in a believer’s journey of faith. It symbolizes the washing away of sins and the believer’s commitment to follow Christ. We practice baptism by immersion, as taught in the New Testament, and we welcome those who are ready to take this step of obedience to God.

Q: Does the church offer any outreach programs to the community?

A: Yes, Divine Inspiration Church is deeply committed to serving our community. Our Community Outreach Ministry engages in various initiatives, such as food drives, charity events, and support for those in need. We believe in living out our faith by making a positive impact on the lives of others.

Q: What denomination is Divine Inspiration Church affiliated with?

A: Divine Inspiration Church is a non-denominational Christian church. We are focused on teaching the Bible, following the teachings of Jesus Christ, and creating a welcoming environment for all believers, regardless of their denominational background.
