Faith, Family, and Fellowship

About Us

Grounded in Faith, Driven by Purpose

Our church is rooted in the timeless teachings of the Bible, with a commitment to fulfilling God’s calling for our community. We are united by a mission to serve, a vision for the future, and a passion that fuels everything we do in Christ’s name.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ, equipping believers to live out their faith, and creating a supportive environment where everyone can experience the transformative power of God's grac

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a beacon of light in our community, where people of all backgrounds come together to worship, grow in faith, and impact the world for Christ through acts of love and service.

Our Passion

Our passion is to glorify God in all that we do. We are committed to cultivating a deep, authentic love for Jesus, and sharing that love with our community through worship, service, and discipleship.

Our Community

Building a Strong, Faith-Filled Community Together

Divine Inspiration Church is more than just a place of worship; it’s a community where faith grows and relationships flourish. We strive to support each other in our spiritual journeys, guided by the love and grace of God.
